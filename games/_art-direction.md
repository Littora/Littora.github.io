# Littora Play: distinct worlds

The collection is a quiet, light doorway. Its cream paper, restrained serif type and sage details let the three games carry the personality.

Lightkeeper uses gallery white, restrained violet, fine rules and precise geometric controls. There is no decorative world illustration: the light path itself is the visual subject.

Driftline uses a sunlit 16-bit palette, real low-resolution sprite rendering, stepped island edges, palm trees and rectangular arcade controls. The locally built Tiny Voyager display font uses original 5 × 7 glyph patterns. The pixel identity continues through the moving boat, currents, collectibles, map and sound, rather than stopping at a cover image.

Tide Garden uses warm cream, pale mint, peach, lilac and butter yellow. Its generated storybook illustration sets the scene; the playable corals are distinct, crisp SVG characters with faces. Each coral has a recognizable silhouette and name, and the live board is never baked into a generated image.

## Generated production asset

Built-in image_gen; no CLI fallback. Output saved as `assets/tide-garden.webp`. The generated 1536 × 1024 composition was retained and encoded at WebP quality 90 for page delivery.

Exact prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original landscape illustration for a browser game named Tide Garden, to appear in its cover card and an illustrated sidebar beside the playable board. Create a refined, charming underwater coral garden, bright and airy with a warm ivory / very pale mint water background, peach-pink rounded corals, butter-yellow fan coral, lilac branching coral and sage sea plants. A handful of coral creatures have very subtle little dot eyes and tiny content smiles, designed with personality and restraint. Lovely handcrafted storybook / contemporary Japanese picture-book sensibility, softly painted gouache, clean rounded silhouettes, delicately visible paper grain, sophisticated pastel color relationships, soft sunlight rays and a few translucent bubbles. Close view of a little coral reef with inviting characters nestled across the lower two thirds, luminous open pale water in the upper third. Rich thoughtful illustration, delightful to adults as well as children, tasteful and timeless, no childish stickers or generic 3D plastic. Wide landscape composition around 3:2, edge-to-edge environment. No text, lettering, UI, logos, borders, watermarks, dark ocean, neon colors or interface mockup. This is a finished production illustration, not a design proposal.


## Three familiar classics

- **Numberloom / 2048:** Bauhaus-inspired burnt sienna, mustard, dusty blue and cream; square geometric tiles, heavy sans-serif numbers and a circle / rectangle emblem. Artwork is original CSS, so numbers stay crisp on every screen.
- **Picnic Snake:** cartoon orchard greens, apple-red buttons and warm gingham. The snake, apple, cheeky eyes, leaf and soft lawn are drawn directly on canvas; the card and sidebar illustrations are original SVG.
- **Field Notes / Minesweeper:** ruled ivory paper, olive embossed squares, coloured clue annotations, a field-club stamp and original botanical line drawings. Serif headings and typewriter details create a field-journal identity.

These new game visuals use repo-native CSS, SVG and canvas rather than additional bitmap generations. No external image, font or script is required.
