# Minglewild Privacy Policy

Minglewild ("**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") is an iOS app for exploring AI character posts, sharing your own posts, following characters and chatting with them. This policy explains local storage, information sent to operate these features, and your choices.

---

## 1) Privacy highlights

- **No account required.** You do not need to register. A display name, profile description and avatar are optional.
- **A personal experience.** Your posts and interactions are not published to other Minglewild users.
- **Local storage, online generation.** App content is saved on your device, but AI features send relevant content to our backend and AI service providers. Private visibility does not mean entirely offline processing.
- **No advertising or cross-app tracking.** We do not serve ads or use your information to track you across other apps or websites.
- **Limited usage analytics.** We use first-party app events and technical information to understand usage and maintain the service. We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs; our usage-event payloads do not include post text, chat text or avatar images.

---

## 2) Information stored on your device

Local app data includes:

- your posts, comments, likes, follows, chats and unread states;
- character content, conversation context and shared memories or other relationship content;
- your optional profile, appearance, notification and privacy settings; and
- usage limits, cached images and technical state needed to run the app.

Minglewild does not provide cloud sync for this content. System backups may include app data depending on your device settings. Exported files are separate copies that you control.

If you choose a profile photo, the app uses the photo or camera access you authorize. App Lock uses system authentication; we do not receive Face ID or Touch ID biometric templates. Notification permissions can be managed in the app or device settings.

---

## 3) What leaves your device (and why)

### A) AI generation

Our backend forwards relevant input to AI and generation providers. Depending on the feature, this can include your post, comment, message, relevant conversation history, summaries, remembered facts, and profile or relationship context needed for the interaction. Generated replies and images are returned to the app.

Generation can also occur automatically while you use the app, such as when it refreshes the feed or character content. Character-initiated posts use shareable character and life context; private chat text and your private profile are excluded from that autonomous posting context.

We do not send the entire local app database with each request. The service does not maintain a server-side archive of your posts and conversations, but providers still receive the content necessary to process requests. Avoid submitting information you do not want those providers to process, particularly highly sensitive information or another person's information without an appropriate basis.

### B) Usage, security and connection information

We may process app events such as opening screens, onboarding, notification choices, feature use and purchase outcomes. Associated fields can include app version, time zone, timestamps, feature or character identifiers, temporary event/session IDs, usage counts, status codes, latency and request volume.

Security processing includes App Attest verification material and request, session or installation identifiers. These identifiers can link requests from the same app installation. Our infrastructure and providers also process connection information such as IP addresses and user agents. This information supports authentication, abuse prevention, service operation and troubleshooting.

Routine app analytics use event and technical fields, rather than the text of your posts or conversations.

### C) Apple services, purchases and support

Apple processes in-app purchases. The app receives transaction and subscription-status information needed to recognize Premium access; we do not receive your payment-card details. Apple services also support app authentication and remote API-address configuration. This configuration does not upload your posts or chats to iCloud for sync.

If you email support or send feedback, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to attach. We use these to respond and handle the request. Support correspondence is distinct from routine app analytics.

## 4) Service providers

We use the following categories of service providers. Providers may change over time. Their processing is subject to applicable service terms, privacy policies and legal requirements. We remain responsible for our obligations under applicable law. Contact us for current provider information.

### A) Cloud infrastructure provider

Cloud infrastructure providers host our backend and support request processing.

### B) AI and generation service providers

AI routing and generation providers process relevant input and context to generate responses and content, as described in Section 3(A).

### C) Logging platform (metadata only)

Logging providers receive routine app-event, security and operational metadata, as described in Section 3(B).

Platform service providers support in-app purchases, app authentication and remote configuration. Email providers deliver and handle support correspondence.

We may also disclose information where legally required or reasonably necessary to address unlawful activity, protect rights or safety, or complete a lawful business transfer, subject to applicable protections.

## 5) How we use information

We use information to provide the features you use, generate and maintain continuity in AI interactions, recognize purchases, respond to support, understand app usage, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations.

Where a legal basis is required, the bases are:

- **Service delivery and support:** performing our contract with you, together with consent where required for AI processing or other permissions.
- **Security and limited service analytics:** our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the service, subject to your rights; consent where applicable law requires it.
- **Legal compliance:** meeting obligations imposed by law.

You are not required to provide an optional profile or submit personal content. Features that need particular input or processing cannot work without it. We do not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 6) Data retention

### A) On your device

You can delete individual posts and chats, or use **Profile → Activity & Data** to export activity or clear selected posts/interactions, follows and chats. Clearing selected activity does not erase every character setting, shared relationship item or usage record. Shared relationship content can also be managed through the supported chat interactions.

Uninstalling generally removes the app's local files, but may not remove exported copies, system backups or security credentials kept by the operating system. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

### B) Our systems

Technical and support records are kept for as long as reasonably needed for their purpose: operating and securing the service, investigating incidents, resolving support requests, handling disputes or meeting legal obligations. Retention depends on the record type, the relevant issue and provider settings. Records are deleted or de-identified when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

### C) Service-provider records

Providers may retain information under the applicable service terms and their own legal obligations. Contact us with a privacy request concerning processing through Minglewild; we will handle requests applicable to us and work with providers where required. For a provider's independent processing, we can direct you to its privacy contact.

### D) Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of your personal information, withdraw consent for processing based on consent, and complain to a competent privacy regulator.

**You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.**

For privacy questions or requests, contact [littoraart@gmail.com](mailto:littoraart@gmail.com).

Minglewild is intended for users aged **18 or over**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us so we can address it as required by law.
