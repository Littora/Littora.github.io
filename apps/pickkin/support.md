## A few useful answers

### An AI nudge won’t load

AI nudges need an internet connection. Check your connection and try again. Failed or canceled replies don’t use up a successful nudge from your daily allowance.

### I want to restore Premium

Open the Premium screen in Pickkin and use the restore option. Make sure you’re signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Subscription management is also available through your iPhone’s Settings.

### Where are my saved decisions?

Open **My decisions** in the app. Your saved decisions live on your iPhone and don’t currently sync between devices. Removing the app can remove this history.

### How do I change the language?

Open **Settings → Language**. Choose one of the seven supported languages or follow the system language. New AI requests use your selection as the default language.

## Tell us what happened

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Pickkin version, and a short description of the problem. A screenshot is useful if it doesn’t contain anything private. Please leave out personal decision details and security credentials.
