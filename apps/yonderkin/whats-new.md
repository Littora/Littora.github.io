# What’s New
## Version 1.0.0 — Welcome to Yonderkin

**Meet the Unimagined.**

Yonderkin is an interactive universe of 32 distinct beings, each with its own identity, world, interests, and style of conversation. Browse every profile—from an alien cartographer and a windwise horse to a living ocean and a library between stars—then start chatting with whoever interests you.

### Explore Yonderkin

- **Meet 32 distinct Yonderkin:** discover their backgrounds, interests, current status, and Moments.
- **Start with a clear direction:** Today’s Signal, World Window, Impossible Door, Dream Exchange, Pocket Universe, and other modules offer new ways into a conversation.
- **Follow what they share:** like or comment on Moments, or continue directly in Chat.
- **Create content worth returning to:** conversations can form memories, plans, dreams, time capsules, future postcards, newly named words, constellations, and shared worlds.
- **Personalize your experience:** choose a Primary Yonderkin, select a theme and App Icon, and use optional App Lock for added privacy.

Choose a Yonderkin and start chatting.
