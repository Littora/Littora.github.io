# Momentlet Support

If you have a question, encounter a problem, or want to share feedback about Momentlet, email us at [littoraart@gmail.com](mailto:littoraart@gmail.com?subject=Momentlet%20Support).

## Helpful information to include

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

- what happened and what you expected;
- whether the issue occurred while creating, viewing, saving, or sharing a Moment;
- your Momentlet version, iOS version, and iPhone model; and
- a screenshot, if it does not contain anything you would rather keep private.

Please do not send personal descriptions, generated images, or other sensitive content unless it is necessary to explain the issue.

## Quick answers

**A failed creation should not use your allowance.** A creation counts only after a valid image is saved to the local Moment library.

**Your Moment library is local to your device.** Momentlet does not currently provide cloud sync, backup, or cross-device recovery.

**Subscriptions are managed by Apple.** You can restore purchases in Momentlet or manage an active subscription in Apple ID Settings.
