# Momentlet Privacy Policy

The full Momentlet Privacy Policy is being prepared for publication before the app becomes available on the App Store.

## Current product principles

- Momentlet asks for permission before sending your description and chosen visual feeling to its image service and AI providers.
- Finished Moments are stored in the local Moment library on your device.
- Momentlet does not currently provide cloud sync, backup, or cross-device recovery for the Moment library.
- Saving to Photos and sharing happen only when you choose those actions.

For privacy questions, email [littoraart@gmail.com](mailto:littoraart@gmail.com?subject=Momentlet%20privacy).
