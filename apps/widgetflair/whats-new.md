# A little flair begins here.

<p class="wf-document__lead">Thoughtful little widgets. Playful new possibilities. Your kind of day.</p>

WidgetFlair is getting ready for its first App Store release. This is a preview of the current collection; downloads will open here when the app is available.

## In the current collection

- **Free designs to make your own.** Start with Studio clock, Soft digital, Calendar tile, Little note, Small victories, Water ritual, Gratitude jar, Pocket companion, Daily perspective, and A little more today. Preview every design for free.
- **A collection beyond clocks.** Discover reading shelves, illustrated window clocks, habit gardens, mood flowers, travel tickets, playful choices, an aquarium, and tiny daily companions.
- **Browse by your kind of day.** Explore Clocks, Dates, Productivity, Wellbeing, Playful, Inspiration, and Progress, or start with a curated set: The sunroom, Quiet rituals, Little adventures, or The desk club.
- **A personal palette.** Choose Cream, Clay, Butter, Lavender, Forest, Midnight, Sky, Peach, or Rose. Make designs in small, medium, or large Home Screen sizes.
- **Little actions, right on your screen.** Compatible widgets support water logging, checklists, habit check-ins, focus timers, mood choices, reading progress, and playful picks. Write and keep gratitude notes inside the app.
- **More ways to make it yours.** Customize words, photos, dates, time zones, personal goals, and time formats where supported. Save designs in My widgets, mark favorites, and share a picture of a design.
- **A place on your Lock Screen.** Compatible designs support inline, circular, and rectangular formats; availability depends on the design and device.
- **Offline by design.** Browse, edit, save, and use widgets without an internet connection. No account, ads, or app analytics. Your widget library stays on your device.
- **Premium, made to stay.** Create and customize the full collection. Saved designs remain usable and editable offline after a subscription ends.
- **Your language, your appearance.** Choose English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, or Norwegian Bokmål inside Settings, or follow the system. System, Light, and Dark appearances are supported.

## More little possibilities ahead

We’ll continue adding new widget designs, ideas, and collections. Premium includes new Premium designs added while your subscription is active. We don’t promise a fixed update schedule or a fixed collection size.

Purchasing and restoring Premium require an internet connection through Apple’s App Store. A photo stored only in iCloud may need to download before offline use. iOS controls widget refresh timing.
