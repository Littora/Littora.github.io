# A little flair begins here.

<p class="wf-document__lead">Introducing WidgetFlair: thoughtful little widgets for your kind of day.</p>

WidgetFlair is being prepared for its first App Store release. This is a preview of the first collection; the download button will open the App Store after approval.

## In the first collection

- **Eight free designs.** Studio clock, Soft digital, Calendar tile, Something to anticipate, Daily perspective, Little note, Water ritual, and A little more today.
- **Six palettes to make them yours.** Cream, Clay, Butter, Lavender, Forest, and Midnight, with small, medium, and large Home Screen sizes.
- **Widgets that fit your rhythm.** Choose your own words, countdown date, time zone, and time format. Log a glass of water directly from a compatible widget.
- **A place on your Lock Screen.** Compatible designs support inline, circular, and rectangular formats on iPhone.
- **Offline by design.** Browse, edit, save, and use your widgets without an internet connection. No account, ads, or app analytics.
- **A little more with Premium.** Flip clock, Photo frame, Across time zones, and Year in dots. Saved Premium designs keep working after a subscription ends.
- **Seven app languages.** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, and Norwegian Bokmål.

Purchasing and restoring Premium require an internet connection through Apple’s App Store. A photo stored only in iCloud may need to download before offline use.
