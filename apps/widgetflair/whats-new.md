# What’s New
## Version 1.0.0 — Introducing WidgetFlair

**A little flair. Every day.**

Beautiful widgets for everyday essentials and little joys. Make your iPhone feel like you.

### In this first release

- **Find your flair.** Explore clocks, calendars, pocket gardens and playful companions.
- **Make it yours.** Choose a palette and add your words, photos, dates or goals.
- **Make yourself at home.** Save your favorites and add compatible widgets to your Home or Lock Screen.
- **Enjoy it offline.** Create, personalize and use your widgets without an account or ads.

Start with a widget you love.
