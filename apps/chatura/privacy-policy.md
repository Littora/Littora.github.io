# Chatura Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11

This policy explains how Chatura and its related services (the **Service**) handle personal information.

Chatura’s operator and data controller is YOUGEN NAN.

Privacy contact: [littoraart@gmail.com](mailto:littoraart@gmail.com)

## 1. Information and purposes

### Local records

No App registration is required for the current Service. The App stores your display name, optional "About Me" text and profile picture, conversations, settings, saved memories, plans, time capsules, wishes and private notes locally. It also stores generated character information, summaries and relationship context used for continuity. The App does not provide its own chat cloud-sync service. Device backups are managed separately by your device and backup settings.

**Local storage does not mean that the information stays on the device during AI processing.**

### AI processing and continuity

The App sends content through our backend to external AI processing services to generate replies and images, maintain conversation memory, update characters and create related content. Depending on the feature, the content includes your message, relevant earlier dialogue, conversation summaries and retained facts, your display name and "About Me" text, character and relationship context, selected or recent saved items, plans and their progress, comments, and relevant dates, language and timezone. Textual image references or addresses associated with a conversation may also be included.

Continuity processing can reuse information from earlier interactions and generate summaries or inferences about them. It is not limited to the latest message. Some AI requests are triggered automatically while you use the App, including proactive character messages, memory summarisation and character updates, rather than by a new message or a separate generation button. Generated summaries and updated context are saved locally and can be sent again for later processing.

#### Sensitive information in content

Sensitive personal information is not required to use the Service. Please avoid including health information, religious or political beliefs, intimate details, or other sensitive personal information about yourself or another identifiable person in messages, profile text or saved context. Do not submit passwords, financial credentials or identification documents.

If you nevertheless include sensitive information, it can be processed as part of your content by us and external AI processing services to provide the features described above. This includes relevant prior content and derived summaries or inferences used for continuity. You decide what to submit and must have any legal authority needed to disclose information about others.

We do not use AI request or response content for model training, model improvement or human review as part of our AI processing.

We configure the AI routing service to exclude endpoints declared to train on request data, and free endpoints declared to publish prompts. The routing service’s content-improvement permission is disabled. The routing service screens requests for child sexual abuse material; flagged content may be retained for authorised review, reporting and legally required preservation.

Model-hosting services may also use automated abuse checks and permit authorised human review of flagged content. Separately, the AI routing service samples some prompts for automated categorisation used in reporting and model ranking. Its published policy states that the resulting anonymous categories are not linked to an account or user identifier.

### Usage, security and network information

If you enable optional product usage analytics, the App collects and sends usage events to our backend, including opening and backgrounding the App, screens and tabs used, onboarding and permission-prompt outcomes, chat submission and response outcomes, and character-update events. These records include timestamps, timezone, app version and build, session and event identifiers, event sequence, and limited feature metadata. The structured usage events record activity rather than chat text and are grouped by session. We use these analytics to understand feature use and reliability, rather than to identify you or track your device across sessions. Events may wait in a local upload queue until sent successfully, displaced by newer events or removed when you disable analytics.

Separately, our backend processes request timing, status and error codes, latency, request size and token usage, and device-bound security identifiers or fingerprints used for authentication, fraud prevention and correlating operational events. These security and authentication identifiers can identify an installation for those operational purposes. Network infrastructure and content-delivery services also receive standard connection data, such as IP addresses, when handling requests or delivering remote images.

We use operational records to provide and secure the Service and diagnose reliability; optional product analytics helps us understand feature use and improve the App. The App does not display advertising, use an advertising identifier, or implement tracking of your activity across other companies' apps or websites. The App does not enable third parties to collect personal information through Chatura to track your activity over time across different websites. This policy covers the App's processing; linked external websites have their own practices.

### Purchases and device services

The device's marketplace processes purchases and supplies transaction and entitlement information used by the App to enable or restore paid features. We do not receive your full payment card details through that purchase flow. The App also fetches remote technical configuration through a platform cloud service; this is separate from syncing your conversations.

Camera and photo access let you choose or take images for local use. Selecting a profile picture does not itself send that picture to an AI model. Notification permission enables local reminders and character notifications; displayed content may be visible on a lock screen according to your device settings. Optional App Lock uses the device's authentication result; the App does not receive biometric templates.

### Support and privacy correspondence

When you email us, we use your email address, message and any attachments to handle your enquiry or privacy request. Our email service processes the correspondence under its own privacy terms.

## 2. Recipients and processing locations

We use hosting and serverless-computing services to run our backend, AI routing and model-hosting services to generate content, and logging services to process the operational records described above. The device marketplace and platform cloud services process purchase and configuration data as described in this policy.

The data-processing terms for our backend hosting, AI routing and operational logging services require those providers to protect the personal information they process on our behalf and limit how they use it.

Our application backend is hosted in the United States, and the operational logging source is configured to store logs in a European region. To provide the Service, information may also be transferred to and processed in other countries where we or the service providers we use operate. Processing locations can vary with the model and infrastructure used for a request, and data protection laws may differ from those in your country of residence.

For applicable restricted international transfers, the hosting and operational logging services’ data-processing terms provide for relevant data privacy frameworks and standard contractual clause arrangements, with UK or Swiss provisions where applicable. The AI routing service’s data-processing terms incorporate standard contractual clauses, a UK addendum and Swiss adaptations. The email service describes its own international processing and applicable transfer mechanisms in its privacy terms.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, or where permitted by law and necessary to protect the Service, address unlawful activity, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Such a disclosure remains subject to applicable privacy law.

## 3. Retention and removal

Local conversations, saved content, derived character context and settings persist until removed through the relevant App controls or removal of the App's local data. Device backups, exported copies and platform security storage may remain separately. Chatura does not offer a server-hosted archive from which we can restore your local conversations.

Our backend handles conversation text, profile text, prompts and AI responses transiently to complete requests; we do not store that content in its logs, databases or backups.

Optional full input and output logging by the AI routing service is disabled; this does not remove the safety-related retention described above.

Some model endpoints do not retain inference content after processing. Other endpoints may retain content under their safety and abuse-prevention policies; prompts flagged by certain endpoints' safety systems may be retained for up to 90 days for abuse monitoring. External image-generation services may store request payloads and generated image files separately from our backend.

Server-side technical, security and usage-event logs we retain, and any copies of those logs or related analysis reports we keep, are retained for no more than 3 days.

The AI routing service retains request metadata, such as token counts and timing, for usage reporting, model ranking and service operation. These records are separate from our 3-day application logs.

The routing service’s published policy bases retention on its business, legal and regulatory obligations. When the information is no longer required, it provides for deletion or anonymisation.

We delete support and privacy-request emails when the matter is closed.

Where applicable law requires retention, that requirement takes priority over the periods above. A local deletion does not itself erase a device backup, a copy you exported, or information previously transmitted to a recipient.

## 4. Your choices and legal rights

Use **Profile → Chat Data** to export or clear chat records. The clear-chat action also clears the conversation-memory files and pending chat-based character updates associated with those chats. Separately saved items and character context have their own storage; clearing chat is not a promise that every related record has been erased. Use the relevant controls for the item concerned or remove the App's local data where a complete local reset is needed. **Clear Cache** only clears downloaded remote image cache files.

You can turn off third-party AI processing in the App's settings. When it is off, the App stops sending new requests for chat, image generation and background AI processing, including retries that have not yet been sent. You can still view or delete records stored on your device. Turning it off does not undo information already transmitted.

Product usage analytics is off until you enable it. You can enable or disable it in the App's settings. Disabling it stops further optional analytics collection and transmission, clears queued events that have not been sent and cancels unsent retries. This does not disable processing needed to provide or secure the Service.

You can change camera, photo, notification and device-authentication permissions through the device or App settings.

Depending on the law that applies to you and the information concerned, your rights may include access and a copy, correction, deletion, restriction of or objection to processing, portability, withdrawal of consent, and an appeal against a decision on a privacy request. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in that law. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise your applicable privacy rights or raise a privacy complaint with us, email the privacy contact above. If applicable law gives you a right to appeal a decision on a privacy request, use the same email address. You may also complain to your competent privacy regulator.
