# What’s New
## Version 1.0.0 — Introducing Cuddavi

**Meet Cuddavi: a little world where care grows into companionship.**

Give your new companion a name and begin a story together.

### In this first release

- **Welcome Moonling home.** Explore six imaginative species and adopt your first companion for free.
- **Care together.** Feed, play, and rest as your companion’s needs, mood, and bond change over time.
- **Talk beyond the ordinary.** AI-powered conversations reflect your companion’s personality, changing mood, and Shared memories.

Your story together starts here.
